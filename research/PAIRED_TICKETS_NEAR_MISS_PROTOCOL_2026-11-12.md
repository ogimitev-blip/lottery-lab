Date agreed: 2026-11-12. Status: **prospective research experiment, not a validated prediction model**.

## Motivation from draw #88 (2026-11-08)

- The previously proposed ticket: `8, 20, 26, 27, 29, 46`.
- User-reported/verified winning draw #88: `7, 19, 27, 28, 29, 47`.
- They have only two **exact** matches (27 and 29), despite there being a one-to-one assignment in which each ticket number is within ±1 of one winning number: 8→7, 20→19, 26→27, 27→28, 29→29, 46→47.
- This motivated diversified *close variants* rather than always seeking maximum distinct-number coverage. The near miss is retrospective, **not** proof that proximity has predictive value.

## User-approved next-draw strategy

Use **six 6/49 lines** organized as three pairs:

1. Main ticket **A** selected using the frozen historical K22 / flexible pool framework; variant **A′** preserving **four** of six original numbers and replacing **two**.
2. Main ticket **B** with variant **B′** using the same rule.
3. Main ticket **C** with variant **C′** using the same rule.

Prefer replacement values ±1 or ±2 near the replaced values, or alternatives scoring well under the *pre-draw* model. Preserve validity (six unique numbers in 1–49); keep selected replacements and pairings frozen **before** the draw. A and A′ need not be disjoint; overlap is the experimental intervention. Avoid duplicating a full ticket. Control total spend under the user's normal €10 per-draw limit (apply verified price per line), and don't add tickets to recover losses.

## Essential comparison and governance

- The experimental paired strategy is an alternative to, not a proven improvement over, six mutually disjoint lines (36 different numbers).
- Freeze two arms for the same draw before results: **paired 3+3 strategy** and **six-line disjoint baseline**. Same number of lines and stake in each comparison; only the actual user-chosen strategy counts as an actual wager if they confirm purchase.
- Assess counts of tickets with ≥3, ≥4, ≥5, 6 exact hits, and draw-level at-least-one win. Also report the total distinct coverage and overlap, as well as distance / near-miss score **separately** from official exact-hit wins.
- Track results prospectively without modifying any frozen proposals after the draw. Do not optimize substitution distance based on draw #88 after seeing future outcomes. Evaluate across many future draws against fair-draw random null and same-budget control.
- All valid distinct tickets have the same jackpot probability in a fair draw; numerical closeness alone is not an advantage.
